Create ONE clean, photorealistic transparent-background product asset for a Windows mouse configuration application, using the attached real GM3323D mouse photo as the identity reference. The product must be recognizably the SAME mouse: matte black low gaming-mouse shell, broad rounded rear and narrower split front buttons, center black rubber scroll wheel, small DPI button behind the wheel, side-button seam on the left, a continuous slim LED rim around the rear base, and the exact simple angular G-like illuminated mark visible on the palm area in the reference (do not replace with letters GM or a Logitech logo). Show the mouse upright with the front/wheel pointing toward the top of the image and rear toward the bottom, with a restrained slightly elevated three-quarter view like a premium hardware configuration app product render. This is an accurate product depiction, not a redesign; preserve the shell shape, proportions and button geometry from the reference. Neutral soft studio light reveals the actual black material without purple ambient tint. For use in a live RGB preview, make the rear LED rim and the palm mark glow neutral WHITE, with a small clean glow; do not illuminate the entire body. Remove the mouse mat, desk, cable, watermark and all background clutter. Genuine transparent alpha background, no floor or fake checkerboard, no text labels, no drop shadow extending outside the product. Center the complete mouse with all edges intact and about 10% transparent breathing room. Output a high-resolution square PNG product cutout.
